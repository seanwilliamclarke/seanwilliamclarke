## Hi, I’m Sean.

I grew up in Whitehorse, Yukon, and moved to Montréal for McGill, where I picked up a B.Eng. in Bioresource Engineering and a Master of Management in Analytics.

These days I live in Toronto and work as a Senior Analyst on the Client Analytics team at BDC. We measure marketing performance, build data products teams trust, and help people in strategy, risk, economic research, and executive leadership answer questions with data.

The coolest thing I’ve built at BDC is an entity resolution pipeline. It figures out when records from different sources describe the same business, and it’s now being adopted as a shared identity foundation for the bank. Looking at the market through our CRM alone was like looking through a straw: we could see the businesses we already knew, but not much beyond them. Linking records gave us a much wider view of the businesses we could serve.

Between work and a few of life’s curveballs, I realized it’s been a while since I made something of my own outside work. I’d like to change that and share what I make as I go. Follow along if you’re curious, or reach out and introduce yourself. I’m always up for coffee or a walk and a chat.
